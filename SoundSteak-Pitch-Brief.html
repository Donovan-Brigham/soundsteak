Private equity knows what musicians don't: music catalogs are exceptional assets. Blackstone, Sony, Primary Wave, and Hipgnosis are acquiring at scale — buying from labels who retained the masters as a condition of the original deal. The artist made the asset and was paid once. The institution collects forever.

SoundSteak changes who owns the asset at the moment it is created. When an artist signs, an LLC forms simultaneously. Every investor — from the fan who pledged $1 to the professional who traded their fee for equity — holds a documented legal stake in the release. The music earns. The earnings distribute automatically to every stakeholder (SteakHolder) on record. Permanently.

"This record label does what is in the best interest of the artist — because that is what is in the best interest of the investors, and in turn, SoundSteak. That alignment is not a policy. It is the structure."

For Artists
  • Full legal infrastructure at signing — LLC formed, ISRC, IPI, PRO, SoundExchange, and MLC registered before a note is recorded. The business is built before the first session.
  • Retain your ownership percentage. Set before Event One, locked into the LLC, never renegotiated.
  • Non-exclusive. Pursue any deal, any label, any contract at any time. Our scope is one single. Everything outside it is yours.
  • Community votes on who you work with. The hired person decides what they create. No direction from the platform, the investors, or the fans.
  • Stems required at delivery — every instrument, every vocal, isolated. Each stem is a separately licensable product the LLC holds permanently.
  • Journey is adaptive. No gear? The system sources it. Need more time? The timeline extends. The platform meets you where you are.
For SteakHolders
  • $1 buys a real stake in the release LLC — not a token, not a handshake. A documented investment in a legal entity with real terms.
  • The first 300 fans to pledge become Founding SteakHolders — a permanent, timestamped badge that cannot be purchased retroactively. You were there or you weren't.
  • Real-time earnings across every revenue stream — streams, sync, live, merch, AI licensing — tracked and distributed automatically, indefinitely.
  • Governance rights. SteakHolders vote on platform decisions. The people who built the audience have a say in what the platform does with it.
  • Fan Cues: structured signals delivered directly to the artist — weighted by stake, not buried in a feed. Intelligence, not noise.
  • Every product launches as a presale — nothing is produced until demand clears the profitability threshold. SteakHolders get first access to every window, and every purchase flows back through the LLC.
For the Industry
  • Engineers, producers, photographers, directors, promoters — every credit documented at the time of work, paid automatically as the release earns. No chasing. No spreadsheets.
  • Option to take LLC equity instead of a flat fee. The engineer who mixed the record earns from what it earns, indefinitely, if both parties agree.
  • Recording studios, rehearsal spaces, luthiers, gear makers, equipment vendors — all sourced through the platform. Every vendor credited. Every transaction part of the permanent record.
  • AI companies access a licensed, contextually annotated music catalog — and pay the artist directly for answers about their own creative process through the developer console.
  • No external sourcing. If it's needed to make the music, it exists in the system. The entire supply chain is on the platform.

The Differentiators
WHY THIS IS DIFFERENT
What is unusual for the usually bad is usually good.
300 real fans are louder than 300,000 fake ones. Every person on this platform has a verified identity, a real dollar on the line, and a documented relationship to the artist. Provenance cannot be purchased. It cannot be manufactured.
An LLC formed per release at the moment of signing. Every investor holds a real legal entity with documented terms — not a token, not a back-catalog royalty that dissolves when the label gets acquired.
A catalog that appreciates through documentation. Every context task completed, every fan behavior recorded, every question answered by the artist builds a more licensable, more valuable asset. Not a fixed recording. A living catalog.
No comment sections. Ever. Expression happens through Cues — structured signals routed directly to the people who need them. The platform has no mechanism for anonymous noise, toxic loops, or manufactured consensus.
A developer console where AI companies license contextually annotated music — and pay the artist directly for answers about their own creative process. Every opt-in is a distinct, automatic revenue stream.
Regular, scheduled mediation between artist, vendors, and SteakHolders — built into the release calendar. Alignment is a meeting on the schedule, not a crisis response after something goes wrong.
Governance rights that extend to how the platform itself is run. The community that built the audience votes on what the platform does with it. This is not a feature. It is the legal architecture.
A conversational AI that treats every participant as a relationship, not a task list. It reaches out before things go wrong. It turns behavior into signal before a human asks the question. The system already knows.
